Questions

FAQ

Do I need an account to buy?

Yes, a free Mirai Hit Labs account. It keeps your plugins, license keys and installers, free plugins included. You don't need a Mirai Hit Studio account; if you already have one, sign in with it.

Where do I download the plugin after paying?

In My plugins. As soon as the payment is confirmed, the installers for each system and your license key show up there.

How does the license work?

The license is personal: you can use the plugin in your projects, including commercial ones. Reselling or sharing the installer or the key is not allowed.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Credit and debit cards through Stripe's secure checkout, in US dollars. Prices on the Portuguese store are in Brazilian reais, with Pix or card.

Is my system compatible?

Each plugin lists its formats (VST3, AU, AAX…), systems and requirements on its own page. Check them before buying.
